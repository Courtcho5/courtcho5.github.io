Bioengineering · Robotics · Medical Devices

Courtney Cho

I design and build mechanical systems that have to work on real people.

I'm a Bioengineering master's candidate at Northeastern University, where my thesis work involves designing a biomechanical test rig to predict femur fracture in patients with osseointegrated prosthetic implants.

My work sits where mechanical design meets the human body: actuated assistive devices, robotic control systems, and medical simulation hardware. I move between CAD and FEA, embedded control and inverse kinematics, and the user research that tells me whether any of it actually helps. Before Northeastern I earned a BS in Human Centered Design and Engineering from the University of Washington.

I'm currently looking for robotics and medical device roles where hardware has to survive contact with real users.

Medical Simulation

PRACTIV — A Novel IV Arm Trainer

A modular IV training arm with anatomically accurate vein placement, a 3D-printed TPU lattice that mimics soft tissue, and a pressurized fluid system for blood flashback. In a formal usability study against a leading commercial trainer, PRACTIV scored 73.6 on the System Usability Scale versus 49.7, with significantly higher ratings across every realism measure tested.

SOLIDWORKS nTopology Silicone Casting Usability Testing
Robotics

6-DOF Robotic Arm & Inverse Kinematics Controller

A six-axis arm designed in CAD and driven by a Python control application I wrote from scratch. It solves closed-form inverse kinematics for a target position in Cartesian space, compensates the wrist to hold the gripper level, and validates every joint against its mechanical limits before a single command reaches the servos.

Inverse Kinematics Python Serial / Arduino Mechanism Design
Master's Thesis · Research

Osseointegrated Implant Biomechanical Test Rig

Designing and building a mechanical test rig to characterize femur fracture risk in patients with osseointegrated prosthetic implants. Now on its third design iteration, with finite element analysis used to verify structural integrity of the fixture before machining.

Finite Element Analysis Mechanical Testing Fixture Design Experimental Design
Assistive Technology · Team Lead

Wheelchair-Mounted Umbrella

Led mechanical design on a two-year HuskyADAPT project: a servo-actuated canopy that opens at the press of a button, mounted to a wheelchair's existing accessory tracks. Requirements came from an occupational therapist whose clients were cancelling therapy appointments because opening an umbrella meant waiting for a caregiver.
