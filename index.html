01About

I'm a Bioengineering master's candidate at Northeastern University, where my thesis work involves designing a biomechanical test rig to predict femur fracture in patients with osseointegrated prosthetic implants. I am currently completeing a 6-month co-op at Neptune Medical as a Clinical Development Engineer in the Robotics Division.

My work sits where mechanical design meets the human body: actuated assistive devices, robotic control systems, and medical simulation hardware. I move between CAD and FEA, embedded control and inverse kinematics, and the user research that tells me whether any of it actually helps. Before Northeastern I earned a BS in Human Centered Design an Engineering from the University of Washington.

I'm currently looking for entry level roles in the robotics and medical device field.
