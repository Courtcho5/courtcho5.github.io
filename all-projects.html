Devices & Robotics

Medical Simulation

PRACTIV — A Novel IV Arm Trainer

A modular IV training arm built to fix what commercial trainers get wrong: oversimplified anatomy, unrealistic tissue feel, and models that represent only one body type. Validated against a leading commercial trainer in a formal usability study with eight clinicians, and published in Simulation in Healthcare.

Robotics · Controls

6-DOF Robotic Arm & Inverse Kinematics Controller

A six-axis arm designed in CAD and driven by a Python control application I wrote from scratch — closed-form inverse kinematics, wrist compensation to hold the gripper level, and joint-limit validation that refuses illegal poses before they reach the hardware.

Inverse Kinematics Python Serial / Arduino CAD Mechanism Design
Assistive Technology · Team Lead

Wheelchair-Mounted Umbrella

A servo-actuated canopy that opens at the press of a button and mounts to a wheelchair's existing accessory tracks — built so users who lack the grip strength to open an umbrella don't need a caregiver to stay dry. I led mechanical design across two years and five team members.

Team Leadership Arduino Servo Actuation Mechanism Design Accessible Design

Foot Drop Assistive Device for Stroke Patients

A six-week design sprint on an ankle-foot orthosis for hemiplegic stroke patients, taken from competitive teardown through cardboard mockup, CAD, and a tested physical prototype.

Osseointegrated Implant Biomechanical Test Rig

Designing and building a mechanical test rig to characterize femur fracture risk in patients with osseointegrated prosthetic implants, in the Shefelbine lab at Northeastern. Now on its third design iteration, with FEA used to verify the fixture before machining.
